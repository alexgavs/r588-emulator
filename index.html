What this simulator is based on. Taken from the firmware itself: the button threshold (5000 ms — short press toggles Wi-Fi, longer restores factory settings), the Wi-Fi switch behaviour, every settings list and factory default in the app (they are the firmware's own option tables), the recording folders and bitrates, and the voice files and where they are played.

Indicator. One blue LED: it blinks once a second while recording and goes dark when recording stops — confirmed both in the firmware and on the device. The red LED is lit by the bootloader during a firmware update. Note that a stopped recording shows the same dark LED as a camera with no power.

Taken from observation, not yet from the code: the Wi-Fi idle shutdown after a few minutes of driving.
